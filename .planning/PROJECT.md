# Project: Cow Fresh POS

## Overview
A specialized Point of Sale (POS) system for Cow Fresh Dairy, focusing on milk product sales, weight-based pricing, and fresh inventory management.

## Vision
To provide a premium, fast, and reliable retail management solution that simplifies billing and tracks the lifecycle of dairy products from supplier to customer.

## Core Modules
1. **POS Billing**: Fast checkout with weight-based support and dairy quick-filters.
2. **Inventory**: Real-time tracking with expiry alerts and wastage recording.
3. **Product Management**: Support for dairy-specific units (KG, Liter) and batches.
4. **Customer & Loyalty**: Tier-based loyalty system for frequent buyers.
5. **Suppliers**: Record management and purchase tracking.
6. **Reporting**: Daily sales and wastage analytics.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Next.js 16, Tailwind CSS 4, Radix UI, Zustand.
- **Backend/DB**: Supabase (PostgreSQL).
- **Format**: Multi-tenant SaaS architecture.
